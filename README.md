Overview

This system optimizes patient access to dental care and enhances service efficiency by providing a user-friendly platform for online appointment booking and management.

Features

1. Patient Profiles: Maintain detailed patient information.
2. Online Appointments: Book, reschedule, and manage appointments easily.
3. Automated Reminders & Notifications: Keep patients informed about upcoming appointments.
4. Administrative Tools: Enable clinic staff to efficiently manage schedules and track patient engagement.

Purpose

Designed to streamline dental clinic operations, improve patient experience, and ensure effective communication between patients and staff.
