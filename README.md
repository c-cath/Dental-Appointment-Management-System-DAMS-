# Dental Appointment Management System

The **Dental Appointment Management System** is a user-friendly platform designed to optimize patient access to dental care and improve clinic service efficiency by enabling online appointment booking and management.

---

## Features

- Patient Profiles to store and manage detailed patient information.  
- Online Appointment Scheduling for booking, rescheduling, and managing visits.  
- Automated Reminders and Notifications to inform patients of upcoming appointments.  
- Administrative Tools that help clinic staff manage schedules and monitor patient engagement.
